Frequently Asked Questions

What data sources do you use? ▼

We aggregate data from The Racing API (live racecards and results), major racing news outlets, horse form databases, and Reddit/community discussions. Our system processes and scores this data in real-time using our proprietary 8-criteria algorithm.

How does the scoring algorithm work? ▼

Our algorithm analyzes eight key criteria: recent form, class of race, speed ratings, track suitability, jockey performance, trainer record, distance effectiveness, and going preference. Each factor is weighted based on historical success rates, and horses are scored 0-100 with explanation of key factors.

Can I cancel anytime? ▼

Yes. We offer month-to-month subscriptions with no long-term contracts. You can cancel your subscription anytime from your account settings, and you'll retain access through the end of your billing period.

Do you offer a free trial? ▼

Yes. New users get a 7-day free trial with access to all Starter features. No credit card required to sign up. After the trial, you can upgrade to Pro or Elite, or continue with the free tier.

Which tracks and regions are covered? ▼

We currently cover 50+ tracks across UK, Ireland, and select international venues. Pro and Elite tiers unlock multi-region access including additional European and global tracks. Coverage is constantly expanding.

Is my data secure? ▼

Absolutely. We use bank-level encryption (TLS 1.3) for all data in transit and at rest. Your passwords are hashed with bcrypt, and we never store payment information directly. We're compliant with GDPR and maintain SOC 2 standards.
